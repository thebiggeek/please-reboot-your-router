# RFC 9006 — Upstream Provider Blame Protocol (UPBP)

**Category:** Informational / Satire  
**Status:** Awaiting Upstream Update  
**Author:** ISP NOC  
**Obsoletes:** Root Cause Analysis  
**Updates:** Accountability  

---

## Abstract

This document defines the Upstream Provider Blame Protocol (UPBP), a standardized
method for Internet Service Providers (ISPs) to attribute all network problems
to upstream providers regardless of fault location.

UPBP allows rapid deflection of responsibility while maintaining operational
confidence.

---

## 1. Introduction

Customers may report:

- High latency
- Packet loss
- Route instability
- International slowness
- Partial reachability
- CDN issues
- Peering congestion
- Complete outage

UPBP defines that all such issues originate upstream.

---

## 2. Terminology

**Upstream**  
Unknown external network.

**Peering Issue**  
Convenient explanation.

**Awaiting Update**  
No action taken.

**Escalated**  
Email sent.

**Resolved**  
Customer stopped asking.

---

## 3. Protocol Workflow

```
Customer reports issue
        |
        v
ISP checks nothing
        |
        v
"Issue from upstream"
        |
        v
"Escalated to provider"
        |
        v
Waiting indefinitely
        |
        v
Ticket closed
```

---

## 4. Standard Responses

Implementations MUST support:

- Issue from upstream  
- Upstream provider problem  
- Peering congestion  
- International routing issue  
- Awaiting update  
- No ETA available  
- Escalated to upstream  

---

## 5. Infinite Blame Loop

If customer contacts upstream:

Upstream response:

> Please contact your ISP.

Loop continues indefinitely.

---

## 6. ETA Handling

ISP MUST respond:

- No ETA  
- Awaiting update  
- Team working  
- Monitoring  

Resolution not required.

---

## 7. Compliance Requirements

An ISP is UPBP compliant if:

- No local checks performed  
- Upstream blamed  
- No ETA provided  
- Ticket closed  
- Issue persists  

---

## 8. Example Exchange

Customer:
> We're seeing 30% packet loss.

ISP:
> Issue from upstream.

Customer:
> Which upstream?

ISP:
> Checking.

Customer:
> Any ETA?

ISP:
> Awaiting update.

Ticket closed.
