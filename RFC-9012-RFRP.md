## Abstract

This document defines the Router Fatigue Recovery Protocol (RFRP), a standardized
maintenance framework where network devices require periodic rest periods due
to operational exhaustion caused by continuous uptime.

RFRP assumes routers behave similarly to human beings.

---

## 1. Introduction

Customers may operate routers continuously for:

* 24x7 uptime
* Months without reboot
* Enterprise environments
* Datacenter deployments
* Production workloads

RFRP defines this as unsafe operational behavior.

---

## 2. Fatigue Symptoms

Routers suffering from fatigue MAY exhibit:

* Slow internet
* Packet loss
* High CPU
* Low morale
* Emotional exhaustion
* Burnout
* Routing anxiety
