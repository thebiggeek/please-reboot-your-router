## Abstract

This document defines the Backend Visibility Assurance Protocol (BVAP), where
ISP personnel determine customer network health entirely through backend portal
status indicators regardless of actual service availability.

BVAP prioritizes dashboard visibility over customer experience and ensures
operational confidence through permanent green indicators.

---

## 1. Introduction

Customers may report:

* Internet down
* High latency
* Packet loss
* Frequent disconnects
* Complete outage
* Routing instability

BVAP defines that customer observations are secondary to backend visibility.

## 4. Approved Backend Metrics

The following metrics are considered authoritative:

* Green dot
* Device online status
* Last heartbeat
* Dashboard uptime
* Ping from same rack
* Internal graph

Customer usability is OPTIONAL.

---

## 5. Standard Responses

Implementations MUST support:

* Backend showing stable
* Device online from backend
* No alarm observed
* Connectivity visible from our side
* Link looks fine internally
* Everything normal in portal

## 7. Compliance Requirements

An ISP is BVAP compliant if:

* Dashboard trusted over customer
* Green indicators override reality
* No external validation performed
* Backend considered absolute truth
