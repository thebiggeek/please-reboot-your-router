## Abstract

This document defines the Maintenance Window Retroactive Notification Protocol
(MWRNP), a standardized procedure for declaring outages as planned maintenance
after service restoration.

---

## 1. Introduction

Customers experience:

- Internet down
- No connectivity
- High latency
- Packet drops
- BGP reset
- Fiber outage

After recovery, ISP MUST declare maintenance.

---

## 2. Protocol Workflow

```
Network outage occurs
        |
        v
Customers complain
        |
        v
ISP silent
        |
        v
Service restored
        |
        v
"Planned maintenance"
```

---

## 3. Notification Timing

| Event | Notification |
|------|--------------|
| Before outage | None |
| During outage | None |
| After recovery | Yes |

---

## 4. Standard Messages

- Planned maintenance
- Service improvement

- Emergency maintenance
- Optimization activity
