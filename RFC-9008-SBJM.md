# RFC 9008 — Shared Bandwidth Justification Model (SBJM)

**Category:** Informational / Satire  
**Status:** Peak Hour  
**Author:** ISP Capacity Planning  
**Obsoletes:** Dedicated Bandwidth  
**Updates:** Customer Expectations  

---

## Abstract

This document defines the Shared Bandwidth Justification Model (SBJM), where ISP
explains reduced performance by attributing bandwidth usage to other users.

---

## 1. Introduction

Customer reports:

- Slow speed at night
- Low throughput evenings
- Intermittent congestion

ISP MUST explain shared bandwidth.

---

## 2. Standard Explanation

> Bandwidth is shared in your area.

---

## 3. Time Based Speed Model

| Time | Speed |
|------|------|
| Morning | Full |
| Afternoon | Medium |
| Evening | Slow |
| Midnight | Full |

---

## 4. Blame Targets

- Neighbors
- Heavy users
- Streaming
- Downloads
- Local usage

---

## 5. Compliance Requirements

- Peak slowdown  
- Shared explanation  
- No upgrade  
- No fix  
- Ticket closed  

---

## Example

Customer:
> Speed drops every evening.

ISP:
> High usage in your locality.

Customer:
> This is business plan.

ISP:
> Shared bandwidth.

Ticket closed.
